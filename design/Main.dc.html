02 — Method

We go to the source, not a database.

Aggregated data is used to corroborate, never to conclude. That is the difference between a search result and a verification.

01

Submit

API, bulk upload, or a link the applicant completes themselves. Consent captured with the case.

02

Source

The request goes to the issuing authority, registrar, institution or employer of record.

03

Adjudicate

Automated where the source is machine-readable, analyst review where it is not. Discrepancies are raised, not buried.

04

Record

A timestamped record naming the source of every line — exportable, and defensible at a tribunal.

05 — Questions

Asked before every contract

How fast is a background check?

Age verification typically completes in 15 minutes, a driving licence in 30, and employment history in 60. Checks that depend on a court or registrar respond at that body's own pace — those turnarounds are listed per country.

Which countries do you cover?

More than 120. Coverage differs by check type, so each country page lists what is available, what it costs and how long it takes.

How is applicant data protected?

Certified to ISO/IEC 27001 and 27701, reporting under SOC 2 Type II. Consent is captured before any check begins, data is held in [REGIONS], and the retention schedule is published.

Do you work with government authorities?

Yes — health, immigration, manpower, education and trade authorities, including national licensing programmes. Tender documentation on request.
